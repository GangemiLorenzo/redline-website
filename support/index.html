Questions

Is the money real?

No. All money in Redline is simulated and has no value, and no real trades are made.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Redline, go to Settings, and under Delete data tap Delete, then confirm. Your account, name, ratings, matches and trades are removed from the server at once. Cannot open the app? Email lorenzo.gangemi.developer@gmail.com from the address of your account and it is deleted within 30 days.

How do I play with a friend?

Pick a mode, choose Against a friend, and share the match code. Your friend enters it in the app to join. To practise without an opponent, choose Train alone: it is not rated.

I forgot my password

There is no password reset yet. Email us from the address of your account and we will help. Guest players have no password, so create an account in Settings to keep your progress.

What do Long, Short and leverage mean?

Long wins when the price rises, short wins when it falls. Leverage (1x to 5x) multiplies both gains and losses on your stake. If a trade loses its whole stake it is liquidated.
