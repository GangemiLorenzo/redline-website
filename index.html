How a duel works

Share a code

Pick a mode and send the match code to a friend.

Trade the hidden chart

Go long or short with up to 5x leverage. You both see the same candles. The symbol and date stay hidden.

Highest balance wins

You both start with 10,000. When the clock ends the chart is revealed and your ratings update.

Pick a mode

Classic

Replay a real price chart from the past, one candle at a time.

Live

Trade BTC, ETH or SOL as the price moves right now.

Train alone

No opponent and no rating. Practise at your own pace.

Each mode keeps its own rating. Redline is a game: the money is simulated and has no value. Nothing here is financial advice.
